# Xapp Show it (test app)

A separate, simple Expo app (`ma.xapp.showit`) for testing the "Show it, say it" idea: the customer shows the load (photo), adds a recorded voice note and/or says the trip in words (keyboard dictation), the AI fills a booking card, drivers offer, and the trip runs to a delivery code. Design: warm white + burnt orange (#C2410C) for customers, ink dark for drivers; Plus Jakarta Sans, Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic text. Keep this look.

- Everything is stored on the phone (`src/store.ts`, AsyncStorage); switching Customer/Driver in Settings (`store.role`) tests both sides. Two example offers (clearly labelled) arrive after sending, and an accepted example driver moves the trip on by itself.
- AI: `src/ai.ts` calls Claude with the Anthropic TypeScript SDK using an API key the tester enters in Settings (kept on the phone only). No key means the card is filled by hand. A real launch needs a backend so no key ships in the app.
- Vehicles: `src/vehicles.ts` (smallest first, with capacity) and 3D renders in `assets/vehicles/`, drawn by `scripts/render-vehicles.py` (Blender `bpy`: `python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install bpy`, then `venv/bin/python scripts/render-vehicles.py <dir> <ids>` and `python3 scripts/pack-vehicles.py <dir>`). Includes a 2-door pickup, a Transit-type large van and a Dongfeng-Captain-type light truck (common in Morocco); prices per type in `src/lib/pricing.ts`.
- Voice notes: `useVoiceRecorder` / `VoicePlayer` in `src/ui.tsx` (expo-audio).
- APK: `.github/workflows/showit-apk.yml` builds on pushes touching `apps/showit/**` and publishes the `showit-apk` pre-release.
- Taken from the older Xapp app (4a67d80): Moroccan place search (`src/lib/places*.ts`, `place-search.ts`, `cities.ts`, `geo.ts`), distance and fair-price estimate (`src/lib/pricing.ts`), and the interface sounds (`assets/sounds`, `src/sounds.ts`). New parts in `src/extras.tsx`: `PlaceField`, `WhenPicker`, `HelpRow` (helpers + riding along), `NoticeBanner` (top alerts with sound), `MyTrips`.
- Browser preview: `npx expo export --platform web --output-dir <dir>` then `python3 scripts/build-preview.py <dir> <out.html>`; in the claude.ai preview the AI uses the page's own Claude (publish with `capabilities: {sample: {images: true}}`, or it can't see photos; `images` is passed as a list), no key needed.
- Languages: `src/i18n.ts` (English, French, Arabic; `useT()` gives `t`, `lang`, `rtl`, `route`). Arabic flips phones to native right-to-left (`I18nManager.forceRTL` + one restart); never write a bare `textAlign` left/right: use `sideText` / `autoDir` (`composer.tsx`), see Round 4. Every new text goes in all three dictionaries.
- The request card (`customer.tsx` `CardScreen`, step 2 "Details"): the photo with "Your load" under it (items, weight, vehicle, care tags, as agreed on "What we saw"; changed only through Edit, which goes back there and returns), then only what is still open: route, time, the helpers card, notes for the driver (the `Composer` message bar only, see Round 5), price. The price row is always − price + (`direction: 'ltr'`, also in Arabic), holding a button keeps stepping (`useHoldRepeat`), and tapping the price lets you type it. Amounts use a no-break space between thousands (`dh` in `shared.tsx`) so "1 990" never shows as "990 1" in Arabic. There is no voice step in this version. Places are picked with `PlacePicker` (`extras.tsx`, see below).
- Time: clock and calendar wheels from the original app (`src/wheels.tsx`: drum animation, tick sound + haptic per row); `when` is 'now' or "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm", shown with `formatWhen`.
- Big microphone (`src/voice.tsx`, expo-speech-recognition) is kept for later; the customer flow has no voice step in this version.
- Before pushing: `npm run typecheck` and `npx expo export --platform android` must succeed.
- Map behind every screen (`src/map/`): `MapBackdrop` (phones: MapLibre with the OpenFreeMap "liberty" style like apps/mobile, labels hidden so they never run under text; `.web.tsx`: still pictures in `assets/map/` made by `scripts/render-map.js`, because the preview can't load tiles, draggable). The top buttons and the map strip under them stay fixed (`App.tsx` `Root`); only the panel below scrolls, and its words vanish at the strip's edge. The strip is live (drag / pinch on phones) with a "show the trip again" button (`recenter`); it shows you (`PersonPin`, from the phone's location; the preview shows a labelled example), then the pickup, destination and road route (`useRoadRoute`, OSRM demo server, straight line when unreachable). The map under the panel sits under a veil (warm white for customers, ink for drivers). Screens tell it the trip with `setMapTrip`. The test-version notice lives in Settings.
- Show your load: photos only (no video in this version). The customer takes as many as they like (up to `MAX_PHOTOS`, 6) with the shutter or picks several from the gallery (`capture` in `flow.tsx`); they line up under the camera with ✕ to remove, and the AI starts only on "Analyse N photos", reading them together ("the same load from different angles, count each thing once"). Requests keep `photoUris` (all photos) for the driver (`PhotoStrip` in `shared.tsx`).
- Customer flow (`src/flow.tsx`, run by `CustomerSide` in `customer.tsx`): 1 `ShowStep` (question, 3-step bar Show it · Details · Send, camera frame with the tray of photos and "Analyse N photos", gallery, "Continue without photo") → `ScanStep` (careful AI reading of all photos, `scanLoad` in `src/scan.ts`) → `SawStep` "What we saw" (items with − / + counts, "not sure" marks, Add item; weight range; vehicle; "Handling the load" tags: fragile, very heavy, needs covering; floor and, above ground, "is there an elevator") → straight to the request card (`draftFromChecked`). No map until the request is sent; then the map fills the screen framing pickup and destination with a radar, and the offers float over it at the bottom (swipe between offer cards; a compact "Request sent" card under them; nothing to scroll). Without a photo or AI, "What we saw" starts empty to fill by hand. `matchPlace` understands "Neighbourhood, City" and is used at send time for typed places.
- Typing with suggestions: search boxes (`PlaceSearch`, `AddItem`) open as in-app layers (`Overlay` / `OverlayHost` in `src/overlay.tsx`, not RN `Modal`, whose separate Android window hides the keyboard height) with the box right above the keyboard (`useKeyboardInset` in `ui.tsx`) and the suggestions stacked above it, best match nearest the box, from the first letter. `PlaceSearch` also asks OpenStreetMap/Photon online (`lib/geocode.ts`, from 3 letters; scripts other than Arabic/Latin are dropped; picked addresses are kept short as "Name, City" and added with `rememberPlace`); "Add item" suggests from `MORE_ITEMS` in `choices.ts`. A "go on" button (`Btn icon="arrow-forward"`) puts the arrow after the words, pointing the way the language reads.
- Helpers have their own card (`HelpSection` in `extras.tsx`, same look as the other cards): how many people load and unload (count only, no price shown), and who goes with the goods (no one / me / someone else with name and phone, sent to the driver as `riderName`).
- Photo reading takes its time: `scanLoad` uses the careful tier (`askAi(..., 'complex')`, preview `modelTier: 'complex'`, API effort high) and reads every photo, in batches of what one call allows (`aiPhotosPerCall`), each batch adding to the list. The scan screen says what it is doing and which photos.
- While a sent request waits for offers, the map fills the screen (`setMapBig`), framed on the pickup and destination (the phone map is rebuilt per trip with `initialViewState` bounds, like apps/mobile) and a radar pulses from the pickup (`Radar` in `map/pins.tsx`, `setMapRadar`).
- The app always opens on the first page (customer: Show it; driver: loads). The customer's first page shows only the question (at the top), the step bar and the camera; a request in progress is announced once when the app opens as a passing notification (`nt_tripNow`). The driver's loads page shows a job in progress as an `OngoingCard` (`shared.tsx`) with Open. A trip or job opens by itself only when new during the visit (just sent / just accepted).
- Notifications (`NoticeBanner` in `extras.tsx`, sent with `notify` in `store.ts`): float just under the top buttons over the page title, with a sound; they leave by themselves after 10 seconds, fly off when swiped left or right, and a tap opens the request or job (`openTrip` / `onOpenTrip` in `src/home.ts`).
- The main `ScrollView` in `App.tsx` lets touches through to the map (`pointerEvents="box-none"`) only while the map is big; on Android a ScrollView set that way can't be scrolled.
- Back button: `useBack(active, onBack)` in `src/back.ts` (Android back via `BackHandler`; in the browser preview one guard history entry while there is somewhere to go back). Steps go back one at a time, trips/jobs back to the first page, and every layer, picker, confirm and settings closes with back. Register new pages and layers with it.
- Navigation (customer): `CustomerSide` keeps a trail of the pages visited; back returns to the page before (`goBack`), the step bar can be tapped (`stepNav` in `flow.tsx`: Show it, Details once there is a load, Send for the request in progress), and the X logo (`goHome` in `src/home.ts`) goes to the first page with a "continue / start over" card. The language button opens a list (Arabic, French, English). In the browser, the camera button opens the camera directly (`cameraInBrowser` in `flow.tsx`). Place suggestions are limited to about two fifths of the screen and scroll inside, starting at the best match right above the box.
- Waiting for offers: the cards at the bottom report their height (`setMapPanel`), and the big map frames the trip in the space left above them.
- Top bar (`App.tsx` `TopBar`): X logo (home), three tabs **Messages · Offers · Profile** (`TabBar` in `src/tabs.tsx`, same rounded switch look, badges for unread messages / current offers), language, Settings. A tab opens `TabPage` over the screen under the bar (the request in progress stays mounted underneath); back, the X or tapping the tab again closes it. Messages: one box per conversation (`threadsFor`), opening `ChatThread` with the box above the keyboard; unread counts via `unread` / `markSeen` in `store.ts`. Offers: Current / History for both sides (customer: their requests; driver: their offers and jobs, incl. answering a customer's counter-price). Profile: customer (photo, name, phone, city) and driver (photo, name, phone, vehicle, plate, papers: licence, carte grise, insurance, CIN — photos stay on the phone, not checked). A page opens from anywhere with `openTab` in `src/home.ts`; a tapped message notice opens its conversation.
- Settings (`src/settings.tsx`, an in-app page in the current colours): use the app as Customer or Driver and Appearance Light / Dark (both as normal-size rows with a radio mark, `Choice`), language, sounds on/off (`store.sounds`, checked in `sounds.ts`), Help and support, Terms of service and Privacy policy (pages left empty on purpose until the texts exist), and the test-version tools (API key, clear data).
- Appearance (`store.appearance`, `paletteFor` in `theme.ts`): four palettes, customer light (`customer`) / dark (`customerDark`, ink with the orange kept) and driver light (`driverLight`, warm white with ink) / dark (`driver`). Light by default for both sides; Settings shows Light · switch · Dark on one line, and the language chips always read English · Français · العربية (left to right). Use `useColors()` everywhere; never import a palette directly in a screen.
- Keyboard (`src/keyboard.tsx`, react-native-keyboard-controller; `keyboard.web.tsx` for the preview): Android draws the app under the system bars, so the window no longer shrinks for the keyboard. `KeyboardShell` wraps the app, `KeyboardScroll` (the main panel, profile, settings) scrolls the focused box fully above the keyboard, and `useKeyboardInset` gives layers (place picker, chat, sheets) the keyboard's height so their box sits right on top of it. Every new typing box must live in one of these.
- "What we saw": all photos in one row, same size; a dark "Retake" pill with a circling arrow; every weight is called an estimate (`kgEstimate`), and Edit opens `WeightEditor` (− / + / typing) storing the customer's own `checked.weightKg` (used by `checkedKg`, the vehicle suggestion and the card).
- Request card: `RouteFields` (`extras.tsx`) is the highlighted From/To card (orange border and "Required" until both are set, pulsing empty ends). Each end opens `PlacePicker`: a map with a fixed pin in its middle (`map/PickMap.tsx`, `.web.tsx` uses the still pictures), the address under the pin (`reverseOnline`), a search box with suggestions above it, "My location" (`locateOnce`) and "Use this spot". When: Today / Another day open the wheels and only OK keeps the time, which then folds into one line with Change; Now needs nothing. Helpers: − 1 + in English/French, + 1 − in Arabic; the icon is MaterialCommunityIcons `human-dolly` (the preview inlines that font). Notes: no ready-made notes (Round 5); the line «اكتب ملاحظات مهمة للسائق أو سجّل له رسالة صوتية» and the message box, which starts empty (the floor goes with the load as `floorText`) with the hint "Write something for the driver" and moving dots (left in English/French, right in Arabic); mic always on the right.
- Waiting for offers: the map starts wider and zooms in (animated) on pickup and destination, and frames them again when the cards change size. Each offer card has Details (sheet: driver, vehicle, their price against yours), Decline and Accept. Accept asks "Are you sure?". Decline asks "Are you sure?" then offers "Make another offer?" (`PriceEditor`, counter-price stored on the offer as `status: 'countered'`; example drivers agree after a few seconds, real test drivers answer in their Offers tab) or "No, just decline". "Edit your offer" (replacing Raise +50) changes the request's price.

# Xapp Show it (test app)

A separate, simple Expo app (`ma.xapp.showit`) for testing the "Show it, say it" idea: the customer shows the load (photo), adds a recorded voice note and/or says the trip in words (keyboard dictation), the AI fills a booking card, drivers offer, and the trip runs to a delivery code. Design: warm white + burnt orange (#C2410C) for customers, ink dark for drivers; Plus Jakarta Sans, Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic text. Keep this look.

- Everything is stored on the phone (`src/store.ts`, AsyncStorage); switching Customer/Driver at the top tests both sides. Two example offers (clearly labelled) arrive after sending, and an accepted example driver moves the trip on by itself.
- AI: `src/ai.ts` calls Claude with the Anthropic TypeScript SDK using an API key the tester enters in Settings (kept on the phone only). No key means the card is filled by hand. A real launch needs a backend so no key ships in the app.
- Vehicles: `src/vehicles.ts`, nine 3D renders in `assets/vehicles/` (copied from apps/mobile).
- Voice notes: `useVoiceRecorder` / `VoicePlayer` in `src/ui.tsx` (expo-audio).
- APK: `.github/workflows/showit-apk.yml` builds on pushes touching `apps/showit/**` and publishes the `showit-apk` pre-release.
- Taken from the older Xapp app (4a67d80): Moroccan place search (`src/lib/places*.ts`, `place-search.ts`, `cities.ts`, `geo.ts`), distance and fair-price estimate (`src/lib/pricing.ts`), and the interface sounds (`assets/sounds`, `src/sounds.ts`). New parts in `src/extras.tsx`: `PlaceField`, `WhenPicker`, `HelpRow` (helpers + riding along), `Chat` (quick replies), `NoticeBanner` (top alerts with sound), `MyTrips`.
- Browser preview: `npx expo export --platform web --output-dir <dir>` then `python3 scripts/build-preview.py <dir> <out.html>`; in the claude.ai preview the AI uses the page's own Claude (`sample` capability), no key needed.
- Languages: `src/i18n.ts` (English, French, Arabic; `useT()` gives `t`, `lang`, `rtl`, `route`). Arabic flips phones to native right-to-left (`I18nManager.forceRTL` + one restart); never hard-code `textAlign` left/right for a language (Android swaps them in RTL). Every new text goes in all three dictionaries.
- The request card (`customer.tsx` `CardScreen`): titled sections with a one-line instruction each (`Section`); the AI's answers are pre-selected choices marked "AI" (`Chip`, goods, weights and driver notes in `src/choices.ts`) that the customer can change by tapping, typing, or saying a correction ("Something wrong?", which re-runs the AI on the card).
- Time: clock and calendar wheels from the original app (`src/wheels.tsx`: drum animation, tick sound + haptic per row); `when` is 'now' or "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm", shown with `formatWhen`.
- Big microphone (`src/voice.tsx`, expo-speech-recognition): speech is written into the text live (ar-MA / fr-FR / en-US) and, where the phone can keep the audio, saved as the voice note for the driver; phones without speech-to-text record a plain voice note.
- Before pushing: `npm run typecheck` and `npx expo export --platform android` must succeed.
- Map behind every screen (`src/map/`): `MapBackdrop` (phones: MapLibre with the OpenFreeMap "liberty" style like apps/mobile, labels hidden so they never run under text; `.web.tsx`: still pictures in `assets/map/` made by `scripts/render-map.js`, because the preview can't load tiles, draggable). The top buttons and the map strip under them stay fixed (`App.tsx` `Root`); only the panel below scrolls, and its words vanish at the strip's edge. The strip is live (drag / pinch on phones) with a "show the trip again" button (`recenter`); it shows you (`PersonPin`, from the phone's location; the preview shows a labelled example), then the pickup, destination and road route (`useRoadRoute`, OSRM demo server, straight line when unreachable). The map under the panel sits under a veil (warm white for customers, ink for drivers). Screens tell it the trip with `setMapTrip`. The test-version notice lives in Settings.
- Show your load: photo or video (`takeMedia` in `customer.tsx`; videos have no length limit, `videoMaxDuration: 0`), played with `VideoClip` (`src/video.tsx`, expo-video); a still from the video (expo-video-thumbnails) goes to the AI. Requests keep `videoUri` for the driver.
- Customer flow (`src/flow.tsx`, run by `CustomerSide` in `customer.tsx`): 1 `ShowStep` (only the question, the 3-step bar and a camera frame with Photo / Video and the gallery; no map) → `ScanStep` (the AI looks at the photo: `scanLoad` in `src/scan.ts` returns items with counts, a typical weight each and "sure", goods kinds, vehicle, care tags, helpers; found items pop in) → `SawStep` "What we saw" (items with − / + counts, "not sure" marks, Add item with common things in `COMMON_ITEMS`; weight shown as a range from the items; vehicle; care tags; at most 3 questions: floor, lift, is this everything / add a photo, which re-scans and adds) → `SayStep` (big mic / type: route, time, price) → the request card (`draftFromChecked`, then `fillCard` adds what was said). The map strip appears from the card on (`setMapShown`). Without AI, "What we saw" starts empty to fill by hand. `matchPlace` understands "Neighbourhood, City".
